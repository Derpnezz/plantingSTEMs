About Us

Planting the future, one workshop at a time.

plantingSTEMs is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to making science hands-on, joyful, and accessible for kids everywhere.

Our Mission

Bring hands-on science experiences to every student, everywhere.

We want to ignite passion for science and technology in youth by providing free hands-on learning experiences through accessible interactive workshops in underserved communities.

Every workshop is built around one goal: turning curiosity into lasting confidence in science.

Our Vision

A world where everyone can thrive in STEM education

We want to see every child, regardless of their background, socioeconomic status, and neurodivergence, thrive and grow in STEM-related interests and education.

We're just getting started, and we're growing one chapter, one workshop, and one volunteer at a time.

What We Value

The roots of everything we do.

Accessibility

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to explore STEM, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Curiosity

We encourage students to ask questions, experiment, and discover how the world works.

Hands-On Learning

We believe students learn best by building, testing, creating, and exploring.


Community

Strong partnerships with schools, families, volunteers, and local organizations allow us to expand our impact.

Growth Mindset

We celebrate mistakes as opportunities to learn, improve, and become more resilient.

Leadership

We empower students and volunteers to become leaders who create positive change within their communities.


Service

Everything we do is centered on giving back and making STEM education more accessible for the next generation.
