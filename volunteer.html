Volunteer

Lend a hand. Help students grow.

From leading a workshop station to mentoring a chapter, there are plenty of ways to volunteer with plantingSTEMs — including opportunities that count toward MCPS Student Service Learning (SSL) hours.

Student Mentor

Social Media & Design

Good to know before you apply

Anyone who is passionate about inspiring the next generation through STEM. We welcome high school students, college students, professionals, parents, and community members.

MCPS Students

Earn Student Service Learning (SSL) Hours

Montgomery County Public Schools students can volunteer with plantingSTEMs and count their hours toward MCPS SSL requirements.
