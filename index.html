Who We Are

A student-run movement for STEM education

plantingSTEMs strives to become an organization that empowers students to unlock their creative and critical thinking through simple and enjoyable hands-on workshops. We're growing into a network of chapters and volunteers to make STEM more accessible for our students in every community.

Whether it's planting ideas at a workshop or organizing a new chapter with our gracious volunteers, our work is powered by students helping students.
